Privacy Policy for BizLedger Pro

Effective Date: October 8, 2026

At BizLedger Pro ("we", "our", or "us"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal and business financial data. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles your information.

1. Information Collection and Storage

BizLedger Pro operates entirely locally on your device. All financial records, transaction histories, categories, and balance sheet data that you input into the app are stored locally using your device's browser/local storage mechanism (such as LocalStorage).

We do not collect, transmit, store, or process any personal or financial data on external servers, cloud databases, or third-party remote systems.

2. Use of User Data

Because all data remains strictly on your device:

  • We do not use your data for profiling, tracking, or marketing.
  • We do not share, sell, rent, or trade any personal or financial information with third parties.
  • Your data is used solely for the purpose of displaying your monthly financial summaries, ledgers, income statements, and balance sheets within your copy of the app.

3. Permissions and Third-Party Services

The app loads standard frontend frameworks (such as Tailwind CSS, Alpine.js, and Chart.js) via Content Delivery Networks (CDNs) for styling and user interface rendering. These scripts do not track your personal financial inputs. The app does not require unnecessary dangerous permissions (such as contacts, camera, or microphone access).

4. Data Security

Since your financial records reside entirely on your device, the security of your data depends on your device's security measures (such as screen locks and device passwords). We recommend keeping your device secure.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
